❓ FAQ

How do I play?
Guess a number 0–9. You get 3 tries and 15 seconds per try. After each guess you'll know if the answer is higher or lower.
What's the best strategy?
Start with 4 or 5. If told "higher" go to 7, if "lower" go to 2. Binary search — you can always win in 3 tries.
How does Daily Challenge work?
10 matches per day. Your total tries is your score — lowest wins. Ties broken by total time. Scores go to the global leaderboard.
How do I challenge a friend?
Tap Challenge a Friend, pick your secret number, tap Send Challenge. Your friend gets a link and has 3 tries to guess it.
