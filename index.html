❓ FAQ

How do I play?
Pick a number between 0–9. You have 3 tries and 15 seconds per try. After each guess you'll see if the secret number is higher or lower.
What is the Daily Challenge?
You get 10 matches per day. Your total tries across all matches is your daily score — lower is better. Compete on the global leaderboard!
What's the best strategy?
Start with 4 or 5 — they're in the middle. If told "higher", try 7. If "lower", try 2. Binary search style gets you there in 1–2 tries most of the time.
How does Friend Challenge work?
Pick a secret number, tap "Create Challenge", then share the link. Your friend opens it and tries to guess your number in 3 tries!
What happens if time runs out?
That try is wasted — you lose one of your 3 attempts. The timer resets for your next try.
How is the leaderboard scored?
Daily: lowest total tries across 10 matches wins. Ties broken by total time. Quick & Friend: ranked by total wins all time.
Can I play without internet?
Yes! Quick Challenge and Friend Challenge work offline. Daily leaderboard needs a connection to submit your score.
My streak disappeared — what happened?
Streaks are stored on your device. Clearing browser data or switching devices will reset it. Play at least once a day to keep it alive!
